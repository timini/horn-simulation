## Evidence required before admitting an assembly

Record the exact driver variant and any specimen-specific parameters; the horn, chamber, adapter and rear-load geometry; mass convention or a measured complex source model; voltage and calibration; microphone distance and axis; room/baffle/ground conditions; gating, smoothing and the usable band; uncertainty and reuse terms. Preserve the raw source identities and distinguish supplied facts from assumptions.

Select two matched configurations with enough information to predict them without using their target response to fit the model. Freeze prediction inputs and acceptance limits before reading the comparison metrics. Retain failed comparisons and classify unsupported configurations explicitly. A normalized shape-only comparison can test resonances and trends, but cannot qualify absolute output, driver efficiency or recommendation ordering.

The next useful acquisition is a complete source/interface specification for an otherwise calibrated assembly, not another uncalibrated plot. No author has been contacted and no hardware purchase or fabrication has been commissioned.

Experimental design, not a qualified physical assembly. The 300 mm length excludes the driver, mounting adapter and rear enclosure. The STEP describes the internal air volume; it does not include walls, fasteners, a driver adapter or a phase plug.

Predicted performance

At 2.83 V RMS and 1 m on axis, the final 6 mm mesh predicts 4.42 dB peak-to-peak variation across 320–5000 Hz, with a logarithmic-band average of 105.52 dB SPL. No EQ is applied. The in-band range is 103.02–107.44 dB SPL.

What was checked

The broad screen evaluated 700 geometries; a focused 38 mm throat-radius screen evaluated another 175. Fifteen distinct geometries received complete FEM sweeps across the broad and focused searches, including five valid local refinement trials. A sixth refinement trial failed CAD eligibility and remains recorded as rejected. The eight broad-search finalists failed the 6 dB criterion; the focused search resolved that failure.

Model and remaining evidence

The prediction uses a lossless horn, an infinite rigid baffle, 16 axisymmetric aperture modes and a linear driver model from the manufacturer’s 8 Ω parameters. The real cone-to-throat adapter, separated moving/rear air masses, enclosure loading and cone breakup are not characterized. Free-standing mouth diffraction, off-axis coverage, distortion and a maximum-SPL rating are not established by this run.

Existing acoustic benchmarks test parts of the solver. They do not physically validate this particular 6NMB420 assembly. The next physical step is a specified driver adapter and rear arrangement, followed by calibrated response and impedance measurements of the assembled prototype.

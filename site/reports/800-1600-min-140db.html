    How to read this report

    1. Every number is a linear model prediction (FEM horn, lumped driver). Treat it as an experimental starting point to build and measure, not a validated recommendation.
    2. Rank 1 is the highest score among designs that meet the hard limits (ripple at most 6 dB in 800 to 1600 Hz, compression ratio, size and driver limits). A design whose limits are exceeded only because of an estimated front chamber is still ranked, with the exceeded limits listed in its Front chamber column (item 6). The score formula and weights are stated under Rankings. Scores within 0.02 are near ties.
    3. Each candidate is named horn ID + driver ID. Plot legends use the same #rank ID, so every curve maps to one ranking row.
    4. Levels are on axis at 1 m for 2.83 V RMS. |Ze| is the electrical impedance an amplifier sees, in ohms. The throat impedance plot is acoustic, horn only.
    5. Build from the top candidate section: the profile CSV gives the air passage wall radius against axial position in mm (z = 0 at the throat), and the STEP file is the same air volume at true size. Add your own wall thickness. File paths are relative to the folder that holds this report.
    6. The front chamber column states the chamber between diaphragm and throat that the prediction used: supplied, estimated from the cone geometry, or none. Estimated chambers are flagged and do not reject a design on their own.
    7. The rejection table counts why other designs failed and how close they came.
